ViceMaps 3D is powered by real global geospatial infrastructure covering every street, avenue, and skyscraper on planet Earth.

Powered by OpenStreetMap and OpenFreeMap vector tiles. Zoom into New York, London, Tokyo, Paris, Mumbai, or your own neighborhood to see real streets, terrain, and building footprints.

Real-Time Device GPS Location

Tap the "Locate Me" radar button to pinpoint your physical GPS coordinates in real time. The camera smoothly descends to your street in 3D at a 65° sunset angle.

Global Turn-by-Turn GPS Navigation

Calculate live driving routes between any two points on Earth with distance, ETA, and maneuver steps over an animated glowing neon vector path via the OSRM routing engine.
